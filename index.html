Meet Our Chefs

Behind every delicious dish is a passionate chef. At Tamutamu Delicacies, our chefs combine traditional African flavors with modern culinary techniques to create fresh, flavorful meals made with love.

James Baraka

Head Chef

With over 10 years of culinary experience, Chef James specializes in authentic African cuisine and modern dining. His passion is turning fresh, locally sourced ingredients into memorable meals.

Harrie Baraza

Sous Chef

Chef Harrie brings creativity and attention to detail to every plate. She specializes in fresh, colorful dishes and ensures that every meal meets Tamtam's high standards.

Marion Mwangi

Pastry & Catering Chef

Chef Marion specializes in pastries, desserts, and large-scale catering. His creativity and precision make him an essential part of our catering team.
